Where my garden is kept, and iCloud

Your save is a file in Mutabloom's own storage on your iPhone. It is written as you play, so there is no save button and nothing to lose by force-quitting.

If you are signed in to iCloud with iCloud Drive on, the game also keeps a copy in the private iCloud container iOS gives it, in your iCloud account. That is what carries the garden to a new phone: sign in to the same Apple Account, install Mutabloom, and it picks up where the last device left off. We cannot read that file — see the privacy policy.

A few things worth knowing about sync:

  • It needs a network. If you play offline on one device and then on another before the first has been online, the two gardens have genuinely diverged, and the game keeps the more advanced one rather than silently discarding progress.
  • It is not a backup service. If you delete the app and also delete its iCloud data, the garden is gone and we cannot recover it, because we never had it.
  • You can turn it off in iOS Settings → your name → iCloud, and the game will keep the save on the device only.

Restoring a purchase

Open Settings → Restore Purchases inside the game. Purchases are tied to your Apple Account, not to a device or to any account of ours, so this works on any device you are signed in on and after any reinstall.

If a purchase does not come back, check that the device is signed in to the same Apple Account you bought it with — that is nearly always the answer — then email us with the date of the purchase. Apple's receipt email has everything we need. Refunds are Apple's to give, at reportaproblem.apple.com.

I bought Remove Ads and I am still being offered ads

That is intentional, and it is worth explaining properly rather than leaving you to conclude you were cheated.

Remove Ads removes the ads that interrupt you. It deliberately keeps the ones that pay you.

Gone for good: the interstitials between screens — after the shop, after selling, on leaving the journal — and any banner the game ever adds.

Still there, on purpose: the optional rewarded videos. Doubling a "while you were away" harvest, a thirty-minute growth boost, a shop re-roll, cloud seeding, halving an egg timer, the free daily premium seed, the extra offline hours, the weekly streak freeze.

Those are rewards, not tolls. If buying Remove Ads switched them off, the purchase would quietly take away free gems, free seeds and free boosts from the people who paid for it. Nobody presses a rewarded button by accident: it says what you get, and nothing happens until you press it. A Gardener's Pass works the same way.

If an interstitial appears after you have bought Remove Ads, that is a fault and we want to hear about it. Close the game and open it again first, since the purchase takes full effect from the next launch, and run Restore Purchases if it persists.

Streaks, and how not to lose one

Any harvest keeps the streak alive for that day, including one you tap from a Live Activity on the Lock Screen. You do not have to open the game to keep a streak, and it does not care how much you harvest — one plant counts.

  • Streak Freeze covers a day you miss. You get one free every week, it applies itself automatically the moment a day would otherwise be lost, and you can hold more — buy one with gems, or take one from a rewarded video once a week. You never have to remember to use one; the game spends it for you.
  • Streak Repair is the second chance, for up to 48 hours after a streak breaks, for gems or a couple of rewarded videos.
  • Turn on the Streak reminder in the game's notification settings and it will tell you before the day runs out rather than after.

If a streak broke while a freeze was sitting unused, that is a bug. Tell us the date and we will look.

Offline earnings, and why they stop

Plants keep growing in real time while the app is closed — that part has no cap at all. What is capped is how long your pets keep harvesting for you while you are away:

  • 4 hours by default.
  • 12 hours with an active Gardener's Pass.
  • +4 hours on top, once a day, from a rewarded video.

Come back after two days and you get the cap, not two days' worth. The cap exists so that the game is a garden you visit rather than a spreadsheet that punishes you for having a life, and so that the "while you were away" screen stays a pleasant surprise instead of the only way to play.

The recap on your return always offers one rewarded video to double what you collected. That offer is once per return, and skipping it costs you nothing you had.

Changing your device clock does not create earnings. The game checks wall-clock time against how long the device has actually been running and caps anything implausible.

Egg odds

Every egg that costs gems shows its drop rates in full, on the purchase screen, before you spend anything, and the same table is available at any time from the Pets screen. Common, Rare, Epic and Legendary eggs each have their own published table.

Nothing in Mutabloom is a paid loot box in disguise: gems are earned by playing as well as bought, eggs are one of the things you can spend them on, and the odds are the same whichever way you got the gems.

Turning things off

  • Notifications — Harvest, Streak, Weather and Gift reminders each have their own switch inside the game, and iOS Settings turns off the lot.
  • Live Activities — the garden on the Lock Screen and in the Dynamic Island is opt-in, and the same switch turns it back off.
  • Haptics, motion and sound — all switchable in the game. The game also follows the system Reduce Motion setting on its own, without being asked.
  • Advertising choices — Settings → Privacy Options in the game reopens the consent form wherever one applies to you, and iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking is where you change your tracking answer.

Deleting your data

Deleting the app removes the save on the device. To remove the iCloud copy too, go to iOS Settings → your name → iCloud → Manage Account Storage and delete Mutabloom's data there.

Game Center achievements and leaderboard entries belong to your Game Center account rather than to the app, so they survive deleting the game; Apple's Game Center settings are where you manage them.

For anything else — analytics, crash reports, a request under the GDPR, the UK GDPR or a US state privacy law — email info@crindustries.xyz and say what you want deleted. The privacy policy lists everything that exists to delete, which is a short list on purpose.

Reporting an ad

If an ad shows you something inappropriate, screenshot it while it is on screen, then use Settings → Report an ad in the game to send it to us. Tell us roughly when it happened. Every ad in Mutabloom comes from Google, and passing the report on is what gets that advertiser blocked for everybody playing.

A note on the numbers

The caps, timings and prices on this page are the ones the game launches with. Several of them are tuned from a server without an app update, so if we change one, this page changes with it and the date at the top moves.
