Grow, mutate and collect plants.

The loop

Two to five minutes, and it ends with something you did not expect.

  1. Shop restocks
  2. Buy seeds
  3. Plant
  4. Wait or boost
  5. Weather rolls the mutations
  6. Harvest
  7. Sell, keep, or feed a pet
  8. Better seeds, plots, tools, pets

The shop restocks every five minutes and the weather runs on its own schedule, the same one for everybody, so a Rainbow event is something a garden is caught in rather than something bought. Mutations land on whatever happens to be growing at the time: Golden, Rainbow, Giant, Glowing, Chilled, Wet, Shocked, and the ones each biome keeps to itself. They stack, and a stacked harvest is worth a preposterous multiple of the seed it came from.

Plants keep growing in real time with the app closed, and your pets keep harvesting while you are away, up to a cap. Come back and the garden tells you what it got up to.

What it stands for

The rules the game is built to, rather than a list of features.

Never block the player

No energy bar, no hard paywall, no twenty-four-hour wall to stare at. If you have five minutes, the game has something for you to do with them.

Every session earns an ooh

A mutation, a new journal entry, an egg hatching, a weather front rolling in. Sitting down and getting nothing is a bug, not a difficulty curve.

Generated, not hand-placed

Plants, weather, mutations and biomes come out of data and rules rather than hand-built levels, which is why the garden keeps producing combinations nobody designed.

Ads are gifts, not tolls

The ads worth having are the ones you choose: double a harvest, re-roll the shop, make it rain. Interstitials are rare, capped, and never in your first sessions or in the middle of weather.

Premium feel

Real-time 3D, hand-painted light, haptics you can feel the soil in, and a garden that carries on living on the Lock Screen and in the Dynamic Island while the app is shut.

Cozy, for grown-ups

Made for people who want a garden to potter in, not a homework schedule. It is not a children's app and is not in the Kids Category.

Ready for iPhone Duo

Being built to fit the fold rather than be stretched across it: laid out by size class instead of by guessing at a screen, and adopting Apple's Duo layout APIs as they land. No feature needs a fold — everything below is the same game.

  • Closed — the ordinary phone layout, identical to every other iPhone.
  • Open — the camera frames the whole garden and the shop, journal, pets and quests move into a panel beside it. Nothing you need is behind a modal any more.
  • Tabletop — Terrarium mode. Garden above the fold, controls below it, the camera rising with the hinge angle. Leave it alone for a minute and it dims to an ambient scene with the weather still playing: a desk terrarium that happens to be a phone.
  • Standing — the garden's Live Activity fills the outer display in StandBy, counting what is ready to harvest.
