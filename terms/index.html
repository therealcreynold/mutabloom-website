What Mutabloom sells

The game is free to download and free to play through. It sells four kinds of thing, all of them through Apple:

  • Remove Ads, bought once, which permanently removes the interrupting ads. It keeps the optional rewarded ads available on purpose — Support explains why at length.
  • Gardener's Pass, a subscription, monthly or annual.
  • Gems, the in-game currency, which can also be earned by playing.
  • Permanent extras and cosmetics: extra plots and pet slots, a tool that raises sell values, pots, fences, garden themes and the like.

Purchases are tied to your Apple Account and can be restored from Settings → Restore Purchases inside the game on any device signed in to that account. Refunds are handled by Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com, not by us.

Subscriptions

A Gardener's Pass renews automatically at the end of each period unless it is cancelled at least 24 hours before the period ends, and your Apple Account is charged for the renewal within the 24 hours before it starts. Manage or cancel it in iOS Settings → your name → Subscriptions; cancelling stops the next renewal and leaves the current period running to its end. We cannot cancel it for you, and deleting the app does not cancel it.

What a Pass gives you is listed in the game at the point of purchase, and what it gives is convenience and cosmetics. It is not required to finish anything.
